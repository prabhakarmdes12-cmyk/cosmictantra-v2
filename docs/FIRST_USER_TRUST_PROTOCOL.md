## 1. Usability Objective

Evaluate whether an everyday user with no background in Vedic astrology can seamlessly:
1. Enter their birth details and achieve confidence in their coordinates and timezone.
2. Immediately understand their primary astrological identity (Ascendant/Lagna, Moon Sign, Birth Nakshatra).
3. Find their current life period (Mahadasha and Antardasha) without feeling overwhelmed.
4. Ask one life question to Kashi and clearly understand the "Why am I saying this?" evidence trail.
5. Generate and download/share a personalized Kundli Book.
