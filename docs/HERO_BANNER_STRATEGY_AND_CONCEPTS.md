## 2. Visual Contrast & Legibility Principles

Hero graphics on web/mobile apps frequently fail because **busy background imagery destroys text readability**. To guarantee 100% legibility across desktop and mobile screens:

```
                      HERO VISUAL LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE
                      
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │ [TOP NAVBAR: Brand Logo & Navigation Controls]               │
    ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
    │ LEFT 60%: High-Contrast Editorial Text                      │
    │   • Headline (Cinzel Serif, White/Gold)                     │
    │   • Subtitle (Plus Jakarta Sans)                            │
    │   • Dual Primary CTAs (Parchment & Gold)                    │
    │                                                             │
    │ RIGHT 40%: Focal Hero Visual Artwork                        │
    │   • 16:9 or 4:3 Framed Sacred Asset                         │
    │   • Soft Scrim & Radial Glow (No Text Overlap Collision)    │
    │   • Atmospheric Diurnal Lighting (Subah / Sandhya)          │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Text Scrim Protection:** All text sits over a dark radial gradient scrim (`from-black/80 via-black/40 to-transparent`).
2. **Dedicated Visual Focus (Right 40%):** Artwork is positioned on the right side on desktop or centered below copy on mobile, preventing background clutter from clashing with copy.
3. **Diurnal Ambient Lighting:** Hero lighting dynamically shifts based on sunrise/sunset (Warm Amber for Day vs. Indigo Night for Evening).

## 4. Proposed Carousel & Rotation Strategy

To maximize engagement without confusing new visitors:
1. **Default Hero Visual:** **Concept 1 (Cosmic Now Dial)** — Provides immediate interactive utility on first load.
2. **Auto-Rotating Editorial Carousel:** Rotates through **Concepts 2, 3, 5, and 8** every 6 seconds with smooth cross-fade animations.
3. **Interactive Tab Pills below Hero:** Allows visitors to manually click through the 5 core capabilities (*Kundli*, *Panchang*, *Calendar*, *Kashi Sahayak*, *Scholar Consult*).
