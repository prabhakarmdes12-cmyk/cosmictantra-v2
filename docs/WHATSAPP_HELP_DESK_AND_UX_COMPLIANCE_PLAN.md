  - `/pandit/workspace`: Junior Pandit & Senior Scholar consultation cockpit with intake lookup and client chart view.
  - `/admin/sabha-ops`: Operational temple and consultation administration portal.

## Section F: Implementation Plan & Architectural Invariants

### Architectural Invariants:
- `INV_HELP_001`: WhatsApp is a transport/channel in V1, never the system of record.
- `INV_HELP_002`: +91 9972934937 is the canonical public V1 Help Desk destination.
- `INV_HELP_003`: A Help Desk interaction must not be presented as an unlimited free astrology consultation.
- `INV_HELP_004`: The user's actual question must survive the handoff from Help Desk to Scholar.
- `INV_HELP_005`: Verified birth information should never need to be manually re-entered by the scholar.
- `INV_PAY_001`: Paid consultation authorization requires server-verified payment evidence.
- `INV_CALL_001`: Paid consultation time begins only after scholar/customer connection is confirmed.
- `INV_CALL_002`: V1 must not pretend that normal WhatsApp calling supports programmable Chiti-controlled transfer.
- `INV_ASTRO_001`: Voice/help-desk operators and AI must not invent deterministic astrology calculations.
- `INV_PRIVACY_001`: Contact phone identity must not be assumed to equal consultation-subject identity.
- `INV_AUDIT_001`: Every payment, assignment, consultation-state and privileged override must be auditable.
- `INV_FUTURE_001`: Business-domain logic must remain transport-independent so WhatsApp can later be replaced or supplemented by Exotel/Twilio/SIP/WebRTC.
