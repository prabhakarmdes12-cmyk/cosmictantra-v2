## How to classify what you find

This distinction matters more than the findings themselves. Please use
exactly these five:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CALCULATION ERROR** | The arithmetic or astronomy is wrong. A longitude, a boundary, a date that does not follow from the stated method. |
| **RULE ERROR** | The calculation is right but the rule applied is not the rule as the tradition states it. |
| **TRADITION DIFFERENCE** | Both are defensible; your school reads it differently. We will record it, not "fix" it. |
| **INTERPRETATION DISAGREEMENT** | The fact is right, the meaning given is not the one you would give. |
| **PRESENTATION PROBLEM** | The content is right and the page is wrong — layout, legibility, ordering, wording. |

A TRADITION DIFFERENCE is not a defect. Two of the eleven yoga rules are
already reported NOT_CALCULATED precisely because we could not choose between
schools, and we would rather extend that list than guess.

## What we are not asking

Please do not read this as a request to add yogas, doshas or vargas. Those are
recorded as future scope and will be handled separately, each with its own
boundary fixtures.

## What we already know is missing

Drishti, Shadbala, Ashtakavarga, Jaimini, Ashtakoota, Gochara, and fourteen
of the sixteen vargas are computed internally but **not delivered and not
verified**. The report says so on page 17. If you expected any of them, that
is useful to know — but their absence is deliberate, not an oversight.
